Privacy Policy

Curvely · last updated 3 August 2026

Curvely is a graphing calculator that runs entirely on your device. It has no account system, no analytics, and no server to send anything to.

What we collect

Nothing. Curvely collects no personal data, no usage data, and no device identifiers.

What stays on your device

The equations you type are held in the app while it is open so it can draw them. They are never transmitted anywhere.

Third parties

  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No data is sold or shared, because none is collected.

Children

Curvely is safe for all ages. Since no data is collected, nothing is collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated date above will change with it.
